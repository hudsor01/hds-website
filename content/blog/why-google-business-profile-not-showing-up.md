### Category Mismatch and Verification Loops

Picking the wrong primary category is the fastest way to kill visibility. Google matches search queries to your listed categories before it weighs anything else. If you run a plumbing business in Denton but list yourself as a general home services contractor, your profile will never surface for urgent drain repair searches. The algorithm sees the mismatch and pushes you down. Picture an HVAC company in McKinney listed under residential maintenance instead of heating and cooling repair. It will miss the urgent searches that matter most to its business.

Verification creates another trap. Google flags profiles that look automated or spammy. Using virtual office addresses, PO boxes outside your service radius or rapid-fire category changes triggers manual reviews that can suspend you for months. I build verification workflows that match your physical footprint, service areas and phone routing exactly to what the business operates. You submit clean data once. Workato routes updates across directories and your CRM so Google never sees conflicting signals.

### NAP Consistency Across Your Tech Stack

Name, address and phone format matters more than most owners realize. Google crawls your directory listings, your website footer, your booking system and your CRM. Formatting quirks like (214) 555-0198 versus 214.555.0198 are not the real risk. An old address on Yelp, a tracking number on one directory or a suite number that only appears in your footer is. Conflicting details make Google less confident that your listing is accurate. Less confidence means a weaker position in local results. I track this with Power BI dashboards that pull directory data, scrape your site footer and cross reference it against your HubSpot contact records. Any mismatch gets flagged automatically.

You need a single source of truth for your location data. I set up Salesforce custom objects or HubSpot company records that store your canonical NAP, service radius and operating hours. Every time you update a holiday schedule or add a new location in Allen, the automation pushes the change to Google, Yelp, Apple Maps and your booking platform. Consistency stops being a manual chore and becomes a background process. Google rewards stable data with better local pack placement and higher conversion rates from map clicks.

### The Review Velocity Trap

Reviews are not vanity metrics. They are behavioral signals that tell Google how real customers interact with your business. A pile of old reviews does less for you than a steady flow of new ones. A steady stream of recent reviews with specific keywords and location mentions pushes your profile ahead of stagnant competitors. Say you run a roofing company in Fort Worth. Automating post-job review requests and routing feedback into a sentiment tracking workflow keeps that stream steady without anyone having to remember to ask.

You do not need to buy reviews or ask friends to post them. That strategy burns your profile fast when Google detects unnatural patterns. You automate the request at the moment of service completion, route positive feedback to your public listing and pull negative feedback into a ticketing system for immediate resolution. Google and every prospect reading your profile can see whether you reply, how fast you reply and how your ratings are spread. Handle feedback like an operations team, not a marketing afterthought.
